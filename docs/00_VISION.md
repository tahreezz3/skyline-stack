# 00 · Vision

## One line
Stack a skyscraper one swinging block at a time; precision builds tall towers, and tall towers build a city.

## Player fantasy
"I have a steady hand." Short, tense, satisfying runs. The tower sways when you get sloppy and
snaps solid when you nail it.

## Pillars (every feature must serve at least one)
1. **One tap, real skill.** The only input is a tap. Timing and reading the sway are everything.
2. **Readable physics, generous forgiveness.** A tiny snap window corrects near-perfect drops; collapses
   only take the unstable top, so a mistake costs progress, not the whole run.
3. **Momentum feels great.** Perfect streaks fill a combo bar that pays out big; juice, sound and
   music ramp with it.
4. **Your city remembers.** Every finished tower adds population and changes the city map, so short
   sessions add up to long-term progress.
5. **Fair monetisation.** Never interrupt a drop. Rewarded ads are optional (continue, double reward);
   one "remove ads" purchase. No energy gates in the core mode.

## Audience and platform
- Casual and mid-core mobile players, all ages (target PEGI 3 / Everyone).
- Portrait, one hand, sessions of 1–3 minutes.
- Android first (Google Play), iOS second. Offline-playable.

## Scope for version 1.0
- Tower round (core loop) with 4 block types and progression.
- City map meta layer (place finished towers, unlock new districts).
- Results, stats, local save, settings, English plus 2–3 languages.
- Rewarded ads, a remove-ads purchase, privacy consent, analytics.

Explicitly **out of scope for 1.0** (backlog): idle city management mode, puzzle mini-games,
online leaderboards, social features. See `04_ROADMAP.md` "Later".

## Success criteria for launch
- Day-1 retention ≥ 35% in soft launch, average session ≥ 2 min, crash-free ≥ 99.5%.
- Store rating ≥ 4.3 from the first 200 reviews.

## Non-negotiables
- Clean room: no assets, code or text from any other game (`07_LEGAL_CLEANROOM.md`).
- 60 fps on a mid-range 2021 Android phone.
